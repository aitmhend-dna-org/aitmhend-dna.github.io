### Added
- Google Analytics 4 behind a cookie question, in English, French and Arabic. Nothing loads from Google Analytics until the reader accepts; Refuse and Accept are the same button, side by side. Accepting sets host-only `_ga` cookies for 13 months with every advertising feature off; refusing, or withdrawing later from the new Cookies button in the footer, turns counting off and deletes them. The answer is remembered for six months. A new privacy page in each language (`privacy.html`, `fr/privacy.html`, `ar/privacy.html`) says what is counted, how to withdraw, and which other services the pages use (#106)

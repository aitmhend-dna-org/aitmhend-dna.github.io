Souss-Massa, Morocco

Who were we, really?

Trace the two lines New here? Start here

One Amazigh (Berber) family from the Souss valley carries two ancestries that could hardly be more different. One never left Africa. The other crossed the sea from Iberia before farming reached Morocco.

Two lines out of a thousand

A Y-chromosome and a mitochondrion are two threads out of the many thousands that make up an ancestry. They are not an ethnicity, they are not a nationality, and they cannot tell you what anyone looked like or believed.
